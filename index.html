Childhood

I GREW UP AT A LARGE TABLE, TOGETHER WITH MY BROTHERS,
IN A HOUSE WITH INTERNATIONAL GUESTS AND A KITCHEN WHERE DISHES FROM ALL OVER THE WORLD WERE SERVED.

I STARTED COOKING AT THE AGE OF 4, WAS ALLOWED TO TRY EVERYTHING I WANTED. OPEN FIRE, SOURDOUGH, PIZZA, PAELLA, sausage, croissant, chinese, JAPANESE, ITALIAN and of course swabian dishes.

Baseball

Baseball is my second passion. I used to practice five days a week — with stints in the German and Swiss national team programs. At the Stuttgart Reds I worked my way up from the youth teams into the second team, at times even filling in for the first team and supporting as a catcher. Today, my focus has clearly shifted towards cooking though.

IN 10TH GRADE, WE HAD TO DO A WEEK OF WORK EXPERIENCE. I WENT TO THE YOUNG, COOL FINE DINING RESTAURANT “IM KÜNSTLERHAUS.” AFTER THAT, I DIDN'T WANT TO GO TO SCHOOL ANYMORE, I WANTED TO STAY IN THE KITCHEN. MY PARENTS FORCED ME TO FINISH HIGH SCHOOL. I'M PROBABLY GRATEFUL TO THEM FOR THAT. WHENEVER I COULD, I SPENT AS MUCH TIME AS POSSIBLE IN THE KITCHEN. AS SOON AS I GRADUATED, I STARTED WORKING FULL-TIME AT THE KÜNSTLERHAUS. THAT'S MY HOME BASE, WHERE I CAN LEARN EVERYTHING AND DO INTERNSHIPS IN OTHER KITCHENS WHENEVER I WANT.

I'M ESPECIALLY GRATEFUL TO COCO KULD, SEBASTIAN WERNING, HANS-PETER WÖHRWAG AND OTTO GEISEL. THANKS FOR YOUR SUPPORT!
